Fall 2026 catalog update View changesHide changes

Highlights since the April 27, 2026 SEOS snapshot.

  • Field schools. EOS 300, 400 and 401 now share school-permission, summer scheduling, fall application, priority and fee notes. EOS 401 also recognizes BIOL 311 and PHYS 441 as prerequisite alternatives.
  • Physics of Climate. EOS 433 has a revised title and description, is cross-listed as PHYS 433, and no longer lists school permission as an alternative to its atmospheric-physics prerequisite or corequisite.
  • Prerequisites and contact hours. EOS 450 now requires EOS 201 or GEOG 376. EOS 450 and 460 change from 3-3-0 to 3-0-0 contact hours; EOS 460 requires EOS 335 rather than the former EOS 335/430 choice.
  • Course coverage. EOS 451 is absent from the fall catalog. The SEOS snapshot now covers 46 EOS courses, 143 supporting courses and the same 19 programs.
  • Program requirements. Climate Science adds first-year math and physics alternatives. The combined physics programs revise upper-year requirements and add PHYS 329. The Ocean Sciences minor adds explicit chemistry, math and physics requirements and expands its elective choices.

Course details

Introductory algebra-based survey of physics including heat, sound, wave motion, light, electricity, magnetism, and modern physics. Life Science applications.

Notes

  • Not open for credit to students registered in or with credit in any of PHYS 102, PHYS 111, PHYS 112, PHYS 125, PHYS 130.

Recommended preparation

  • One of MATH 100, MATH 102, MATH 109 recommended prior to or in conjunction with PHYS 102B.
